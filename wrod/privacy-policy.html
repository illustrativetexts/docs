Privacy Policy

Short version: wrod doesn't collect, store, or transmit anything.

What the app does

wrod lets you type a word or short phrase and displays it as large as possible on screen, for showing to someone else. That's it.

Data collection

wrod does not collect any personal data. Specifically, the app:

  • requests no device permissions
  • makes no network or internet connections
  • includes no analytics, advertising, or tracking libraries
  • does not save the text you type anywhere — once you close or leave the app, it's gone

Third parties

None. wrod doesn't talk to any server, service, or third party, because it doesn't talk to anything at all.

Children's privacy

Since wrod collects no data from anyone, this applies equally to users of any age.

Changes to this policy

If this ever changes — say, a future version adds a feature that involves data — this page will be updated first, and the update date below will change.
